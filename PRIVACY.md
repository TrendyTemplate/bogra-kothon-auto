# Privacy Policy - Bogra Kothon Auto

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Bogra Kothon Auto is an internal publishing tool used by the news portal "Bogra Kothon" (Facebook page: facebook.com/bograkothon) to publish its own news posts to its own Facebook Page.

## Data we collect
The tool does not collect, store or sell personal data of visitors or users. It only reads public news headlines from news websites' RSS feeds and publishes posts and comments to the Bogra Kothon Facebook Page, after approval by the page administrator.

## Facebook data
The tool uses a Facebook Page access token belonging to the page administrator solely to publish posts and comments on that Page. No Facebook user data is collected, shared or stored.

## Data deletion
Because no personal user data is stored, there is nothing to delete. For any question or request, contact: mrkarimdesignxpress@gmail.com
